Since 2017

One oven,

The story of Crumb & Ember is mostly flour, fire and stubbornness.

How it started

A secondhand mixer and a pile of bricks.

In 2017 we traded a reliable paycheck for a secondhand spiral mixer and a summer of sore arms, building a small brick oven in the back of a rented storefront on Mill Lane.

We had one rule then, and it still holds: nothing leaves the counter that we wouldn't happily eat standing up, still warm, straight from the bench.

The first winter we sold eleven loaves a day. Now we sell out by ten — but the first bake still goes to whoever queued outside in the dark.

What we stand on

Four things we'll never compromise

  • Fire

    Everything meets real flame. It costs us time and buys us flavor.

  • Flour

    Stone-milled regional grain, milled weekly. We know the farmers' names.

  • Patience

    Cold rooms, long ferments, no shortcuts. Flavor is just time made edible.

  • Community

    Day-old goes to the neighborhood kitchen. Coffee's free if you need it.
