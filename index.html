Our story

One oven, lit every morning at four.

Crumb & Ember began with a secondhand mixer, a brick oven we built ourselves, and a stubborn belief that neighborhood bread should be extraordinary.

Nine years on, we still shape every loaf by hand — and the first bake still goes to whoever's queued outside.
