Good to know

Frequently asked, honestly answered

How does pre-order pickup work?

Submit by 8 PM and we'll bake your items into tomorrow's schedule. Collect any time after opening — your bag is labeled and waiting on the shelf behind the counter.

Do you take custom cakes?

Gladly. Tell us date, servings and vibe at least 5 days out. We'll sketch flavors with you — Midnight Chocolate is the crowd-pleaser, but honey-lemon converts people.

Anything gluten-free or vegan?

A rotating GF loaf bakes Wednesdays and Saturdays; vegan morning buns appear on Sundays. Cross-contact happens — we won't pretend otherwise, severe allergies beware.

Wholesale for cafés and restaurants?

We supply a handful of neighborhood cafés daily. Email hello@crumbandember.example with volumes and delivery window.
