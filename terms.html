Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies worldwide

1. Who we are

Crumb & Ember is an artisan bakery at 12 Mill Lane, Old Town. These terms apply to our website, pre-orders placed through it, and purchases made in-store or via WhatsApp. By using this site or placing an order, you agree to them.

2. Pre-orders

  • Pre-orders submitted by 8 PM (local time) are scheduled for baking the following business day.
  • An order is only confirmed once we reply by email or WhatsApp. If we cannot fulfil an item — the oven has moods — we'll offer a substitute or a full refund.
  • Unclaimed pre-orders are held until end of day and then donated; no refund applies.

3. Prices & currency

  • Prices are shown in US dollars (USD) for consistency across countries. Your bank or payment provider converts to your local currency at its own rate.
  • Custom cakes and bulk orders are quoted individually before any payment is taken.

4. Payment

We accept cash on pickup, card in-store, mobile money, and bank transfer for wholesale. For shipped orders, payment is collected in full before dispatch. We never store your card details on this website.

5. Shipping & delivery

  • Pickup: free, from 12 Mill Lane during opening hours.
  • Local delivery: quoted per order via WhatsApp before you commit.
  • International shipping: available for preserved goods (cookies, granola, celebration kits). Fresh bread and pastry do not ship — they deserve better. Import duties, taxes and customs delays in your country are your responsibility.

6. Allergens & food safety

We bake with wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts and other allergens in one kitchen. While we take care with separate prep and clear labeling, cross-contact is possible; we cannot guarantee allergen-free products. When in doubt, ask before you order — we'd rather answer twice than risk once.

7. Cancellations & refunds

Cancel standard pre-orders up to 8 PM the night before for a full refund. Custom cakes require 72 hours' notice. If something reaches you below our standards, tell us within 48 hours — we'll replace it or refund it. That's not policy, that's pride.

9. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim connected to an order is limited to the amount paid for that order. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded, including for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

10. Your statutory rights

Nothing in these terms limits consumer rights you hold under the laws of your country. Where local law conflicts with these terms, local law wins for you.
