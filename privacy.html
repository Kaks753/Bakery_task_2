Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Compliant with GDPR & friends worldwide

1. What we collect

  • Order details you type into our form: name, email, pickup date, request type, order notes and any allergy information you choose to share.
  • Conversation records if you contact us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Anonymous usage data via our hosting provider (Vercel), such as pages visited and approximate region — no advertising trackers, ever.

2. Why we collect it

One reason only: to bake for you. Your details confirm orders, schedule pickups, flag allergens and reply to questions. We do not sell, rent or trade your information with anyone.

3. Third parties who touch your data

  • Vercel hosts this website (usage logs only).
  • Formspree transmits the pre-order form to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts serves typography (no personal data beyond standard requests).

Each processes data under its own privacy terms; none receive more than needed.

4. Where data lives

Form submissions are forwarded to our email and kept in it. Hosting and email may involve servers outside your country; by using the site you consent to that transfer, which is safeguarded under applicable data-protection frameworks.

5. How long we keep it

Order emails are retained up to 24 months for warranty and accounting purposes, then deleted. Allergy details shared voluntarily are used solely for that order.

6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • show you everything we hold about you,
  • correct anything wrong,
  • delete it entirely ("the oven-forget-me"), or
  • export it somewhere else.

Email mwikalijoyce301@gmail.com and we'll act within 30 days — usually much sooner.

7. Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics profiles. The only thing stored on your device is your browser's own memory of fonts it downloaded.

8. Children

We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. Young bakers are welcome in-store, accompanied by a grown-up account holder.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top moves and material updates are noted right here.
